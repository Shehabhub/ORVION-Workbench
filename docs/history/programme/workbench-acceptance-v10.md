## Intended result and authority

The owner and agents can start and coordinate work through Workbench. Workbench governs its own changes and validates its own coordination operations. For a selected product it invokes that product's Boot, contracts, Gate, Finish and release/certification engines and preserves their actual verdicts. It cannot grant product write authority, replace a failed native check or convert unreadability into success.

Four operational selections must work: Platinum through Workbench; Founder through Workbench; both through Workbench with actual safe concurrency; and Workbench's supervisory tools. Additionally prove each product standalone without Workbench/the other product, and Workbench CORE without either product. Product authorities gain no external dependencies. A selected product's complete native lifecycle through Workbench must be functionally and authoritatively equivalent to direct execution. A launcher or overlapping unit suites alone cannot establish that equivalence.

The measurable target is parity in change admission, assurance and evidence freshness, plus additional coordination, wrong-target and recovery checks. More files, profiles or tests do not establish superiority. Report local, remote, simulated and clean-machine proof separately.
